# Typography

The current bundled fonts use two licenses. ManusCC0 Bold is the full, unmodified
WOFF2 file under CC0 1.0. Figtree, Cinzel, Noto Sans SC and Noto Serif SC are subset
WOFF2 files under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their complete notices are bundled
beside the font files:

| Family | Current use | License and full notice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ManusCC0 Bold | Titles and cost, attack, health, mana and score digits | CC0-1.0: [ManusCC0-CC0.txt](assets/template/fonts/ManusCC0-CC0.txt) |
| Figtree | English body and button text | OFL-1.1: [Figtree-OFL.txt](assets/template/fonts/Figtree-OFL.txt) |
| Cinzel | Headings, card and hero names | OFL-1.1: [Cinzel-OFL.txt](assets/template/fonts/display/Cinzel-OFL.txt) |
| Noto Serif SC | Chinese titles, headings and names | OFL-1.1: [NotoSerifSC-OFL.txt](assets/template/fonts/display/NotoSerifSC-OFL.txt) |
| Noto Sans SC | Chinese body text and fallback | OFL-1.1: [NotoSansSC-OFL.txt](assets/template/fonts/NotoSansSC-OFL.txt) |

See [runtime font sources](assets/template/fonts/SOURCES.md) for the exact current
filenames and [replacement evidence](docs/provenance/fonts/replacement-fonts.json)
for the reviewed ManusCC0 source, full-file hash and size. The full CC0 grant is
also preserved in [the source license](docs/provenance/fonts/manus-cc0-ui-sans/LICENSE).
Keep the applicable bundled notices when redistributing the fonts.

[Historical font evidence](docs/provenance/historical-font-licenses/index.json)
records replaced families and their notices. Those records describe earlier
revisions; the replaced font binaries are not bundled in this revision.
